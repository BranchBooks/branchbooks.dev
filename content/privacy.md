BranchBooks has no server and no account, and it collects nothing about you.

## What the app sends

It connects to the network only for:

- **Sync**, with the git remote you give a book, sending that book to it. A book with no remote is never sent
  anywhere.
- **The update check**, to `branchbooks.dev` and GitLab, when the app starts and when you choose **Check for
  updates**. It asks for the latest version and downloads it; it sends nothing about you or your books. To stop
  the check at start, turn off **Check for updates when BranchBooks starts** in [Settings](/docs/settings/).

It doesn't send anything else unless you ask it to.

## This website

The site has no analytics, trackers or cookies, and loads nothing from other sites.
