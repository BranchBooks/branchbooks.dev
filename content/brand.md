How to show BranchBooks: the logo, the wordmark, the colours and the type.

## Logo

A ledger book: ruled lines on the left page and, on the right, a git branch in a green circle.

<div class="brand-row light">
  <img src="/images/icon.svg" alt="BranchBooks logo" width="96" height="96">
  <img src="/images/icon.svg" alt="" width="48" height="48">
  <img src="/images/icon-small.svg" alt="" width="32" height="32">
  <img src="/images/icon-small.svg" alt="" width="16" height="16">
</div>

- Use the full logo at 48 px and up, and the small drawing, which has fewer and thicker lines, at 32 px
  and below.
- Keep clear space around it of at least a quarter of its width.
- Don't recolour it, rotate it, add effects or put it on a busy picture.

## Wordmark

The logo with the name, set in IBM Plex Mono SemiBold, a nod to plain-text ledgers.

<div class="brand-row light"><img src="/images/wordmark.svg" alt="BranchBooks" height="48"></div>
<div class="brand-row dark-bg"><img src="/images/wordmark-dark.svg" alt="BranchBooks" height="48"></div>

Use the dark wordmark on light backgrounds and the light one on dark backgrounds. Write the name as one
word with two capitals: BranchBooks.

## Colour

Ledger green marks the brand and what you can click. Neutral greys carry everything else. In the app, the
other colours always mean something, so don't use them for decoration.

White text on ledger green has a contrast of 6.5:1, and dark text on the dark-mode green 10.4:1. Both
pass WCAG AA.

## Type

- **Wordmark:** IBM Plex Mono SemiBold, as outlines, so no font is needed to show it.
- **App and site text:** the system's own font (Segoe UI on Windows, Roboto on Android).
